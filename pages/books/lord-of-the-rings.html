Book Description

The Lord of the Rings is a fantasy book series by J.R.R. Tolkien about a young hobbit, Frodo Baggins, who must destroy a powerful ring to save Middle-earth. He travels with his friends and faces many dangers. The story is about friendship, bravery, and the struggle between good and evil.
