Book Description

Harry Potter is a fantasy book series by J.K. Rowling about a young wizard, Harry Potter, who studies at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. He learns magic, makes friends, and fights against the dark wizard Lord Voldemort. The story explores friendship, courage, and the battle between good and evil.
